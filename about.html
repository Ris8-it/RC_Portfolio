About me

I’m Riccardo. I studied finance at Tor Vergata in Rome, did a stint at ING in Milan, a summer at Baird in New York, and some advisory work between Palermo and Milan. Most recently I spent a year on financial management and budgetary reporting at an EU agency in Amsterdam.

What I actually do is narrower than any of that sounds. Behind almost every number an organisation reports there is a person exporting a file and filtering it by hand. I find those people, work out what they are really doing, and build the thing that does it for them. Then I write it all down, because a tool nobody can run after I leave is not a tool, it is a liability with a nice icon.

Three things I believe about this work

Agree the definitions first

The model is the easy part. Getting two departments to accept one definition of a field is the part that decides whether the thing survives contact with a real month end.

Make the right action the easy one

A tool is worth exactly what it gets used. That usually means fewer options rather than more, and making the wrong move hard to do by accident.

Write it down or it does not exist

Everything ships with rendered documentation and a guide. Partly for the team. Mostly because I will forget how it works within about six weeks.
